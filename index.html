01 / A LITTLE INTRO

Curious by nature.
Intentional by design.

I partner with people who believe the best work comes from asking better questions, sweating the small stuff, and making room for a little wonder.

Over the past 6 years, I’ve helped early-stage teams and established brands turn big ideas into thoughtful digital products. My practice lives at the intersection of strategy, visual design, and front-end development.

When I’m not pushing pixels or untangling a tricky interaction, you’ll find me collecting art books, finding new neighborhood coffee shops, or taking the long way home.
